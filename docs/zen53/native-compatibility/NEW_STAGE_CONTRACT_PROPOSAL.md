The prepared stage in [PR191's preparation report](https://github.com/stevengalvis/modelfc/pull/191#issuecomment-6370620243) was consumed by the [single blocked attempt](https://github.com/stevengalvis/modelfc/pull/191#issuecomment-6371781359). Its undeclared `rootfs/root` and provisioning records are evidence. Leave it intact and do not treat it as pristine.

The observed reporter failure was inherited-proc ROOT ENOENT. The [retained-evidence diagnosis](https://github.com/stevengalvis/modelfc/pull/191#issuecomment-6372179929) did not retain the numerical NSpid vector, mappings or contemporaneous pidfd liveness. The proposed reporter adds only a numeric failure-context line before the unchanged fatal marker. The proposed supervisor records an additional bounded snapshot from its existing pinned pidfd/procfd before cleanup. Any absent, malformed or mismatched snapshot is UNKNOWN/MISMATCH and does not change acceptance. The seven O_PATH probes still permit only EPERM/EACCES for inherited proc, and ENOENT only for fresh proc. No sleeps, new probes, wider output budget, policy relaxation or retries are proposed. Output-capture retention remains conditional on pipe/output behavior as before.
